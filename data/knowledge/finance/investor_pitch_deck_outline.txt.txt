Investor Pitch Deck (12 slides)

1. Problem (1 slide): The pain you solve, with data.
2. Solution (1): Your product, key features.
3. Market (1): TAM, SAM, SOM.
4. Business model (1): Pricing, revenue streams.
5. Traction (1): Revenue, users, partnerships.
6. Competition (1): Landscape and your unfair advantage.
7. Team (1): Founders and key hires.
8. Financials (1): 3‑year projections, unit economics.
9. Ask (1): Amount, use of funds, milestones.
10-12. Appendix: demo screenshots, customer testimonials.

Placeholders: $X MRR, Y% MoM growth.

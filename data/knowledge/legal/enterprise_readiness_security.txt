B2B Enterprise Readiness Checklist
1. SOC2 Type II Compliance: 
   - Independent audit of security, availability, and confidentiality.
   - Required for selling to Mid-market and Enterprise.
2. Data Processing Agreement (DPA):
   - Essential for GDPR/CCPA. Defines how you handle customer data.
3. Service Level Agreements (SLA):
   - Define uptime guarantees (e.g., 99.9%) and support response times.
4. SSO (Single Sign-On):
   - Support for Okta, Azure AD, or SAML. Often a "premium" feature.
5. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
   - Granular permissions (Admin, Editor, Viewer).

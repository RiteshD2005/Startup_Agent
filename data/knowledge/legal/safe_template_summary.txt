SAFE (Simple Agreement for Future Equity) – Key Clauses

1. Valuation Cap: $5M – the maximum valuation at which the SAFE converts.
2. Discount Rate: 20% – discount applied to the next round price.
3. MFN (Most Favored Nation): Allows investor to adopt better terms if later SAFEs are issued.
4. Pro‑rata rights: Right to maintain ownership % in future rounds.

Typical SAFE is used for pre‑seed/seed rounds. Always customise with a lawyer.

Content Marketing for Startups

1. Audience definition
   - Create 2–3 detailed personas (job title, goals, challenges, where they hang out online)

2. Channel prioritisation
   - LinkedIn: thought leadership, company updates
   - Twitter/X: product announcements, community engagement
   - Blog: SEO‑friendly how‑to guides and case studies
   - Email: nurture sequences for leads

3. Content pillars
   - Problem awareness (why current solutions fail)
   - Solution introduction (how your product fixes it)
   - Success stories (case studies, testimonials)

4. 30‑day content plan
   - Week 1: Launch blog post + LinkedIn summary
   - Week 2: Customer interview video (15 min) shared on LinkedIn + YouTube
   - Week 3: “Top 5 features” explainer video
   - Week 4: Case study of early adopter

5. Metrics to track
   - Traffic (unique visitors)
   - Engagement (comments, shares, time on page)
   - Leads (newsletter signups, demo requests)

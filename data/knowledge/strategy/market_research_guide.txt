Market Research Steps (Startup Edition)

1. Define the problem and target audience
   - Who has the problem? (company size, role, industry)
   - How painful is it? (cost, time, frustration)

2. Secondary research
   - Industry reports (Gartner, CB Insights, Statista)
   - Competitor websites and reviews (G2, Capterra)
   - Funding announcements (Crunchbase)

3. Primary research
   - Interview 10–15 potential customers (30 min each)
   - Use open‑ended questions to discover unmet needs
   - Validate willingness to pay with “Would you pay $X/month?”

4. Analyse market size
   - TAM (Total Addressable Market): all potential customers
   - SAM (Serviceable Available Market): those you can realistically reach
   - SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): first‑year target

5. Synthesise insights
   - Create 2–3 customer personas
   - Map competitor positioning
   - Identify whitespace opportunities

Output: 10‑page market research deck or Notion summary.

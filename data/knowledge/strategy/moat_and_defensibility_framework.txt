Strategic Defensibility (The 7 Powers)
1. Economies of Scale: Unit costs decline as volume increases. 
   - Strategy: Centralize purchasing or automate infrastructure.
2. Network Effects: Product value increases with each new user.
   - Strategy: Build features that encourage multi-player collaboration (e.g., Slack, Figma).
3. Switching Costs: High "pain of exit."
   - Strategy: Deep integration with client APIs/workflows; high data gravity.
4. Branding: Trust premium.
   - Strategy: Focus on category leadership and "best-in-class" positioning.
5. Cornered Resource: Exclusive access to a valuable asset (IP, talent, or data).
   - Strategy: Patent core algorithms; exclusive data partnerships.
6. Counter-Positioning: A new business model that incumbents cannot copy without hurting their existing business.
   - Strategy: Radical pricing transparency vs. opaque industry standards.
7. Process Power: Embedded organizational knowledge that is hard to replicate (e.g., Toyota Production System).
